# Spotify Bilingual Lyrics V14.1 - Hoc tung cau

## Chuc nang moi
- Nut "Hoc cau" tren thanh cong cu va man hinh loi toan man hinh.
- Chuyen cau truoc/sau, nghe lai tu dau cau (dua vao moc thoi gian LRC).
- Tuy chon an/hien Pinyin va ban dich trong che do hoc, ghi nho tren thiet bi.
- Bai tap dien mot tu duoc chon tu cau hien tai; kiem tra va luu thong ke so lan tra loi tren thiet bi.
- Lap cau A-B bang Spotify seek neu bai co loi dong bo va thiet bi phat ho tro.

## Gioi han
- Lap A-B khong ho tro bai chi co loi TXT khong dong bo, cau cuoi thieu moc ket thuc, hoac khi Spotify khong cho phep seek. Spotify co the co do tre khi tua.
- Khong thay doi toc do nhac: Spotify Web Playback API khong cung cap toc do phat tuy y.
- Bai tap hien tai la dien tu theo van ban, khong phan tich am thanh hay cham diem phat am.
- Khong tai ve file nhac Spotify hoac tu dong chia se loi bai hat.
- Thong ke bai tap duoc luu cuc bo tren thiet bi; chua dong bo Google Drive.
- Chua kiem thu dang nhap va phat nhac tren tai khoan Spotify thuc.
